Our Programs

Explore the variety of programs we offer at Little Hoopers Basketball Training Center

Little Dribblers (Under 5)

Introduction to basketball through fun games and activities that develop basic motor skills and hand-eye coordination.


Mini Hoopers (Under 7)

Focus on basic basketball skills like dribbling, passing, and shooting in a playful and interactive setting.


Junior Hoopers (Under 10)

Structured training with drills and exercises to refine fundamental skills, teamwork, and understanding of the game.


Future Stars (Under 12)

Advanced training focusing on strategy, in-depth skills development, and preparing for competitive play.
